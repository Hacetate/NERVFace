NERVFACE ASSETS
The images and derived glTF files were prepared from user-supplied material.
They are not licensed by the Three.js MIT notice above. The user should confirm
publication rights and author consent before distributing these materials.
The viewer restores source transforms, camera, world and area-light energy from
scene.json. Exported PBR materials are not an exact copy of Cycles shading.
The installed WebGPU glTF BSDF fixes clearcoat IOR at 1.5. Non-default Blender
spread, unsupported color transforms and unconverted SSS are reported in the
viewer. No image-match claim is made. No OIDN, AI denoiser or CDN is used.

LIGHT-CONVERSION REFERENCES (consulted, no Blender source code bundled)
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blender/blender/main/intern/cycles/scene/light.cpp
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blender/blender/main/intern/cycles/kernel/light/area.h
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blender/blender/v4.5.0/intern/cycles/blender/light.cpp
Cycles area_light_eval converts source RGB radiant flux to emitted radiance:
color * powerW / (PI * world-space area), or color * powerW / PI with normalize=false.
Disk/ellipse area is PI/4 times transformed full width times full height.
This is a radiometric shader-input mapping; no arbitrary lumen/watt efficacy or
image-matching gain is applied. The full-spread formula does not reproduce the
soft-box-grid attenuation of a narrower Blender spread.

RELIGHTING HDRIs (assets/hdri/)
Downsampled 1K derivatives of files from the user's local library D:\HDRI Sky
(studio, interior, garden, forest, river, afternoon, cloudy, space, sky).
Original filenames are recorded in assets/hdri/manifest.json. These packs
(e.g. Dosch, HDRI-Hen/HDRI Haven, Alex Hart, Arnaud Lonys, XP HDR) carry their
own licenses; the user must confirm redistribution rights before publishing
this site. The default lighting remains the inherited Blender source scene;
selecting an HDRI hides the source world and area lights.

VERSION-PINNED ADAPTATIONS
The viewer checks Three r187 pipeline error flags because compileComputeAsync
can resolve after reporting shader errors only to the console. Renderer-scoped
subclasses report these failures and drain outstanding work before case disposal.
A tracer-scoped adaptation disposes replaced environment CDF storage and handles
sample-readback failures. No global renderer, material or light prototypes are
modified. Raster LTC nodes and tables are owned by the viewer instance.
